## User Story

As Platform, I want all compliance and underwriter actions to emit structured audit and observability events so that compliance evidence and metrics are available for retention and monitoring.

---

## Business Context

Regulators require auditable evidence of compliance decisions. Observability events also enable operational monitoring of queuing delays, screening latencies, and error rates.

## Implementation Guidance

- **Entity:** `AUDIT_EVENTS`
- **Events:** `audit.event`, `screening.completed`, `task.actioned`
- **Retention:** store event payloads in audit store with 7-year retention; ensure correlation_id present
- **Delivery:** at-least-once; consumers must handle duplicates using idempotency key

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Audit event structure `[integration]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Audit event emitted for screening
  Given a screening completes
  When the service emits `audit.event`
  Then payload contains correlation_id, trace_id, actor, and minimal PII according to redaction policy
```

### AC-002 — Retention policy `[e2e]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Audit event retention verified
  Given the audit store is configured
  When a retention job runs
  Then events older than 7 years are flagged for archival per policy
```
