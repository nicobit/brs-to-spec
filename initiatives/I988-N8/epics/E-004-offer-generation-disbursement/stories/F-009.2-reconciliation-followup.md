## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Flag discrepancy and create follow-up `[e2e]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Reconciliation flags discrepancy and creates follow-up
  Given reconciliation job finds amount mismatch for disbursement id
  When discrepancy is detected
  Then a follow-up task is created assigned to Finance and `reconciliation.flagged` is emitted
```

### AC-002 — Manual amendment `[integration]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Finance corrects a reconciliation record
  Given a flagged reconciliation record
  When Finance submits verified amendment
  Then reconciliation status becomes RESOLVED and an audit event is emitted
```

---

## Business Context

Reconciliation and follow-up ensure financial integrity and give operations a clear process for resolving mismatches, preventing downstream accounting errors.

---

## Implementation Guidance

- Implement reconciliation job with idempotent processing and follow-up task creation.
- Ensure reconciliation records reference disbursement id, provider reference, and custody hash.
