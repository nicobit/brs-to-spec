## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Offer created and sent `[integration]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Create offer and send to DocuSign
  Given an approved application arn
  When the Offer Service creates an offer
  Then `offer.generated` event is emitted and DocuSign envelope created
```

### AC-002 — Acceptance recorded `[api]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Applicant accepts offer via DocuSign
  Given an active DocuSign envelope
  When applicant signs
  Then `offer.accepted` event is emitted and offer status becomes ACCEPTED
```

---

## Business Context

Offers are the legal contract between applicant and lender; the acceptance step must be secure, auditable, and produce immutable records for downstream payment and reconciliation.

---

## Implementation Guidance

- **Entity:** `OFFERS` in implementation contract
- **API:** `POST /api/v1/offers`, DocuSign callback endpoint must update offer state
- **Events:** `offer.generated`, `offer.accepted` — include correlation_id and actor
