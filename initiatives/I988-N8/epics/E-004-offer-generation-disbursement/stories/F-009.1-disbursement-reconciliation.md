## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Disbursement executed `[integration]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Execute disbursement via adapter
  Given an ACCEPTED offer with amount
  When disbursement job runs
  Then disbursement record is created and `disbursement.executed` emitted
```

### AC-002 — Reconciliation `[e2e]`
```gherkin
Scenario: Reconciliation detects mismatch
  Given external provider reports different amount
  When reconciliation runs
  Then reconciliation record flags discrepancy and creates a follow-up task
```

---

## Business Context

Accurate reconciliation prevents financial loss and ensures that accounting has a single source of truth for disbursements.

---

## Implementation Guidance

- **Entity:** `DISBURSEMENTS`
- **API:** `POST /api/v1/disbursements` for execution, reconciliation runs against provider ledger
- **Events:** `disbursement.executed`, `reconciliation.flagged`
