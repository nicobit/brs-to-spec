## User Story

As a **notification service**, I send a submission confirmation to the applicant after successful submission so the applicant has proof of receipt and the ARN.

---

## Business Context

Confirmation emails reduce inbound support queries and provide the ARN for future reference.

---

## Linked Requirements

- REQ-003, FR-004

---

## Implementation Guidance

- Trigger notification on `application.submitted` event. Include ARN and submission summary. Use templated email with i18n support.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Email sent `[integration]`

```gherkin
Scenario: Confirmation email published on submission
  Given an application.submitted event with arn and applicant_email
  When the notification service processes the event
  Then a confirmation email is sent to applicant_email containing the ARN and submission timestamp
```

### AC-002 — Missing email address `[unit]`

```gherkin
Scenario: No email present does not crash service
  Given an application.submitted event missing applicant_email
  When notification service attempts to send
  Then it logs a recoverable error and emits a monitoring alert
```
