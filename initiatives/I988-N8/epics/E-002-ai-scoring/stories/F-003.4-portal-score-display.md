## User Story

As an **underwriter**, I want to see the score and short explanation in the portal so I can make informed decisions quickly.

---

## Business Context

Underwriters need quick access to scoring and a brief rationale to triage applications; this reduces decision time and supports consistent manual overrides when needed.

---

## Linked Requirements

- REQ-014, FR-009

---

## Implementation Guidance

- Display a compact score badge and a collapsible explainability panel showing top contributing factors. Call the scoring API to retrieve score and explainability summary; avoid rendering raw model artifacts.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Score and explanation visible

```gherkin
Scenario: Underwriter views score and summary
  Given an authenticated underwriter
  When they open an applicant record with a score
  Then the portal shows the score badge and an explainability summary with top factors
```

### AC-002 — Explanation access control

```gherkin
Scenario: Explanation access respects permissions
  Given an underwriter without explainability permission
  When they open the applicant record
  Then the explainability panel is hidden and an audit event is emitted
```
