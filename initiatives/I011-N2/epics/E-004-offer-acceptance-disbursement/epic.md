# E-004 — Offer Generation, Acceptance, Disbursement

## Summary

Epic E-004 generates loan offers, captures digital acceptance, and triggers disbursement instructions to the core banking system.

## Business Objective

Deliver a smooth and auditable path from approval to funds disbursement while respecting cooling-off and AML/KYC constraints.
