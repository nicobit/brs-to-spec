# UC-004: Underwriter Review

## Description

Underwriter reviews referred applications and makes a decision.

## Primary Actor

Underwriter

## Main Success Scenario

1. Underwriter opens application from queue.
2. Underwriter reviews applicant data, credit report, AI explanation.
3. Underwriter approves, declines, or requests more information.

## Postconditions

- Decision recorded immutably with actor and timestamp.
