## Preconditions

## Main Success Scenario

| Step | Actor | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Underwriter | Opens application in underwriter queue |
| 2 | System | Presents AI score, credit report, AML/KYC status |
| 3 | Underwriter | Approves, declines, or requests more info and records reason |
| 4 | System | Persists decision and notifies applicant or routes accordingly |
