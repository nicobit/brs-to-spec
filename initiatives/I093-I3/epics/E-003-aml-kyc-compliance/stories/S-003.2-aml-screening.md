## User Story

As the AML Service, I want to screen applicants against sanctions and PEP lists, so that high-risk applicants are identified early.

---

## Business Context

Automated screening ensures regulatory compliance and reduces the number of manual review tasks by filtering low-risk cases.

## Implementation Guidance

- **Entity:** ScreeningResult
- **API:** POST `/api/applications/{applicationId}/screen`
- **Events:** ApplicantScreened, HighRiskApplicantDetected
- **Rules:** Matches to sanction lists create high-risk flags and reviewer tasks

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Sanctions match `[integration]`

```gherkin
Scenario: Applicant matches a sanctions list
  Given an applicant whose identifiers match a sanctions entry
  When the AML screening runs
  Then the system records a sanction match and creates a reviewer task
```

### AC-002 — No matches `[unit]`

```gherkin
Scenario: Applicant clears screening
  Given an applicant with no sanctions or PEP matches
  When the AML screening runs
  Then the screening result is recorded and score < 80
```
