# E-001 — Application Intake (Overview)

This epic establishes the public applicant intake channel: form, validations, ARN assignment, confirmation, and anonymous status lookup. It ensures data captured meets downstream scoring and compliance needs.

## Goals

- Capture complete applicant data for scoring and KYC.
- Provide immediate ARN and confirmation to applicants.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Applicants can submit required fields and receive ARN (FR-001, FR-003).
- Confirmation email sent within SLA (FR-004).
