## Business Objective

Provide regulatory AML and KYC screening to ensure offers are only generated for compliant applicants and to flag compliance holds for manual review.

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- AML screening against HM Treasury lists, HMRC identity verification, compliance hold routing.

**Out of scope:**
- External sanctions aggregation services beyond initial integration.

---

## High-Level Acceptance Criteria
