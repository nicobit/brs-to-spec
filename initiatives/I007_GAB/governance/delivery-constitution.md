# Delivery Constitution — Minimal

This minimal delivery constitution provides Requirement Handling Rules required by the `create-atomic-requirements` skill.

## Requirement Handling Rules

- Preserve source IDs (`OBJ-`, `FR-`, `NFR-`) as canonical identifiers.
- Every `FR-` and `NFR-` in the BRS must be extracted as an atomic requirement in EARS notation.
- Constraints in the BRS are treated as `C-` requirements.
- Open questions are recorded and flagged as blocking when they prevent a clear extraction.
