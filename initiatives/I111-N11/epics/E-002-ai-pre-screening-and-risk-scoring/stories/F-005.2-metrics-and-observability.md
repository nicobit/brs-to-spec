## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Metrics emitted `[infrastructure]`

```gherkin
Scenario: Scoring emits infra metrics
  Given scoring service processes requests
  When scoring runs
  Then latency, success, failure, fallback_count and auto_approve_count metrics are emitted
```

### AC-002 — Alerts `[infrastructure]`

```gherkin
Scenario: Alert on elevated fallback rate
  Given fallback_count exceeds threshold for 5m
  When threshold breached
  Then an alert is raised to on-call with paging rules
```

---

## Business Context

Monitoring SLOs ensures acceptable decision latency and reduces business risk from systemic failures.

## Implementation Guidance

Emit Prometheus-compatible metrics or Application Insights telemetry. Configure alerting rules and dashboards. Ensure metrics include tags for model-version and environment.
