## User Story

As the system, I want to send a confirmation email after a successful submission so that the applicant receives proof of submission and ARN.

---

## Business Context

Provides applicant assurance and an auditable confirmation channel; supports dispute resolution and customer service.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Confirmation email `[integration]`

```gherkin
Scenario: System sends confirmation email after submission
  Given an `application.submitted` event
  When the notification service processes it
  Then an email is sent to the applicant containing the ARN
```

### AC-002 — Email send failure handling `[integration]`

```gherkin
Scenario: Email provider failure
  Given an `application.submitted` event
  When the notification service attempts to send an email and the provider fails
  Then the event is queued for retry and an incident is logged
```
