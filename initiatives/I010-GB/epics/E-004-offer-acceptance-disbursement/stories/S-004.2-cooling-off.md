# S-004.2 - Cooling-off notifications and enforcement

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Title | Cooling-off notifications and enforcement |
| Layers | backend / integration |
| Story Type | feature |

## Business Context

Ensure 14-day cooling-off is enforced and reminders are sent at day 7 and day 13.

## User Story

As the system, when an offer is accepted, I want to schedule cooling-off reminders and enforce the cooling-off window so that disbursement follows rules.

## In Scope

- Schedule day 7 and day 13 reminders
- Enforce 14-day cooling-off before disbursement unless waived

- Integration tests for notification subsystem

## Dependency Contracts

- Consumes: acceptance records and notification service APIs

## Required Tests

- Unit: scheduler correctness

## Gherkin Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
@important @automate
Feature: Cooling-off acceptance criteria

@important @automate
Scenario: Cooling-off reminders scheduled
	Given an acceptance recorded at T0
	When the system schedules reminders
	Then reminders are scheduled for T0+7d and T0+13d

@critical @automate
Scenario: Cooling-off enforcement prevents disbursement
	Given acceptance at T0
	When a disbursement is attempted before T0+14d
	Then disbursement is blocked

@important @automate
Scenario: Waiver bypasses cooling-off
	Given applicant waives cooling-off
	When waiver is recorded
	Then disbursement may proceed

@important @automate-later
Scenario: Reminder delivery failure
	Given notification subsystem failure
	When reminders are queued
	Then reminders are retried according to backoff policy
```
