# Quality Gates

Use these quality gates to decide whether to continue to the next step.

## Gate 1 — Capability Extraction Ready

Continue only if:

- business goals are identified
- personas are identified
- capabilities are business-oriented, not copied from BRS sections
- business rules are captured
- systems and data entities are identified
- source traceability exists
- major open questions are visible

## Gate 2 — Epics Ready

Continue only if:

- every epic maps to one or more capabilities
- epics represent business outcomes
- scope and out-of-scope are clear
- dependencies and risks are visible
- NFR/security/audit/compliance concerns are included
- story groups are identified

## Gate 3 — Stories Ready for Review

Continue only if:

- every story has a parent epic
- every story has acceptance criteria
- stories are not too large
- stories are not purely technical unless explicitly needed
- exception flows are included
- traceability is present

## Gate 4 — Backlog Ready for GitHub

Continue only if:

- backlog review decision is "Ready" or "Needs minor fixes"
- high-severity gaps are closed or explicitly accepted
- clarification issues are created for unresolved questions
- labels and structure are consistent

## Gate 5 — Ready for Implementation Planning

Continue only if:

- traceability matrix is complete
- test notes are present
- architecture dependencies are known
- delivery order is understandable
- blocked items are clearly marked
