# Business Copilot

Business Copilot mode allows business users to run early intake from Microsoft 365 Copilot, Word, SharePoint, Teams, or Copilot Studio.

## Folder

```text
prompts/6-business-copilot/
```

## Use when

Business users should:
- summarize the BRS,
- extract requirements,
- identify questions,
- propose initial slicing,
- review/approve business content,
without using VS Code or the repository.

## Outputs

Recommended SharePoint outputs:
- BRS summary,
- business objectives,
- business requirements,
- gaps and questions,
- delivery slicing,
- next increment scope,
- acceptance expectations,
- approval checklist.

## Rule

Business Copilot output is draft until reviewed by the business owner.
