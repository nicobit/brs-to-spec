## Business Objective

Enable applicants to submit complete loan applications online so the platform can capture lead data, initiate scoring, and start compliance checks. This epic reduces manual intake effort and accelerates application throughput.

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Public applicant portal, intake form, basic validation, ARN assignment, confirmation email.

**Out of scope:**
- Complex integrations, underwriting decision automation (handled in E-002/E-004).

---

## High-Level Acceptance Criteria

- AC-E-001: Applicant can submit form with mandatory fields and receive ARN.
- AC-E-002: Inline validation prevents invalid submissions.
