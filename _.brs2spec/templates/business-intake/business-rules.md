# Business Rules

> **Primary consumer:** Business analyst, PO, architect, QA, AI coding agent
> **Purpose:** First-class list of all business rules extracted from the BRS. Rules are traceable to source, to features, and to stories.
> **Downstream use:** story.md constraints section; traceability matrix; QA test design; AI coding agent safety constraints
> **Do not duplicate:** functional requirements (FR-NNN) — business rules are the *logic and invariants* behind requirements, not the requirements themselves.
