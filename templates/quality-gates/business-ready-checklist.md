# Business Ready Checklist

Do not proceed to engineering contract creation until this is complete.

## Business intent

- [ ] Business objective is clear.
- [ ] Main users / actors are identified.
- [ ] Main process flow is described.
- [ ] Business rules are explicit.
- [ ] Out of scope is clear.

## Requirements

- [ ] Functional requirements are listed.
- [ ] Non-functional requirements are listed or explicitly not applicable.
- [ ] Data requirements are clear enough.
- [ ] Security and authorization expectations are clear.
- [ ] Audit / compliance expectations are clear.
- [ ] Reporting needs are clear or explicitly not applicable.

## User stories

- [ ] Main user stories are defined.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable.
- [ ] Negative/error scenarios are covered where relevant.
- [ ] Role-based behavior is covered.

## Gaps

- [ ] Critical gaps are resolved.
- [ ] Remaining assumptions are accepted.
- [ ] Open questions have owners.

## Epics and features

- [ ] Business objectives are identified.
- [ ] Epics are defined and mapped to business objectives.
- [ ] Features/capabilities are defined and mapped to epics.
- [ ] Requirements are mapped to features/capabilities.
- [ ] User stories reference parent epic and feature/capability.
- [ ] Orphan requirements are identified.
- [ ] Features without requirements are justified or removed.

## Architecture alignment

- [ ] Architecture draft exists or is explicitly not applicable.
- [ ] BRS/requirements vs architecture alignment review completed for medium/large changes.
- [ ] Contradictions between BRS and architecture are resolved or owned.
- [ ] Missing architecture decisions are identified.
- [ ] Data/integration gaps are identified.
- [ ] Security/authorization gaps are identified.
- [ ] Audit/logging gaps are identified.
